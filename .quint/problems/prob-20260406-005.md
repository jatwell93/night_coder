# Select Planner pillar for overnight system v1 (spec-kit vs OpenSpec vs shotgun)

## Signal

The project has selected Orchestration (Ralph), DTU (WireMock), Judge (OpenJudge), and Observability (Langfuse phased + OpenLLMetry), but no Planner pillar is formally decided. Current planning is partly manual and partly Quint-centered, which risks conflating decision memory with execution planning. Without a dedicated planner, transition from pilot to v1 may suffer from inconsistent task decomposition, weak acceptance traceability, and reduced unattended execution reliability.

## Constraints

- Must remain compatible with the chosen Orchestration pillar (Ralph) and must not replace orchestration responsibilities
- Must be open-source or free-to-start for pilot/v1 evaluation and fit overall budget constraints
- Must support machine-usable planning artifacts (spec/plan/tasks/checklists), not just conversational guidance
- Must support headless or CLI-driven usage suitable for overnight automation
- Must preserve provider flexibility and avoid lock-in to a single LLM vendor

## Optimization Targets

- Increase planning reliability and completeness for unattended overnight runs
- Improve traceability from scenario requirements to executable tasks and acceptance checks
- Minimize integration complexity and operational overhead on a solo-operator workflow

## Observation Indicators

- Community star count and social trend velocity (monitor only, not selection target)
- Breadth of optional ecosystem integrations beyond immediate v1 needs
- Opinionated methodology depth that may not materially improve pilot-to-v1 outcomes

## Acceptance

A single Planner tool is selected with documented rationale and fallback. In a controlled trial, the selected planner can generate a complete plan package for at least one representative scenario (requirements/spec, technical plan, ordered tasks with dependencies, and acceptance criteria) that can be executed by Ralph and evaluated by OpenJudge with minimal manual intervention. Integration overhead and runbook changes are documented, and rollback triggers are defined.

## Blast Radius

Primarily planning and execution preparation workflow: runbooks, scenario authoring process, and pilot-to-v1 delivery cadence. Limited direct impact on DTU/Judge/Observability components if planner boundaries are respected.
